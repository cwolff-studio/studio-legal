cwolff-studio

Personal video publishing tool

cwolff-studio is a personal, non-commercial desktop app. Its owner uses it to write, render, schedule and publish short vertical videos to their own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and other platforms. Everything runs on the owner's own computer; the app has no servers and is not available to the public.
