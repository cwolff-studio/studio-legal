Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Social Studio (“the app”) is personal, non-commercial software used only by its owner, Charles Wolff, on the owner's own computer, to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else. Contact: charleswolff721.2@gmail.com.

1. Data the app uses

Only data of the owner's own connected accounts, with the access the owner approves on each platform's sign-in screen:

  • sign-in tokens, to publish on the owner's behalf;
  • the account's name and ID, to show which account is connected;
  • links and IDs of the owner's published posts;
  • like, view and follower counts of the owner's posts and accounts, read only when the owner asks for them and not stored.

The app does not access anyone else's data, messages, contacts or location.

2. Storage and sharing

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Tokens are kept in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager). The app has no servers, no advertising and no tracking. Data is sent only to the platform being published to. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. These web pages set no cookies; the app itself keeps one cookie in the owner's own browser to unlock itself.

3. YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of data from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its token immediately.
  • Access can also be removed on each platform: Google at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Facebook and Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.
  • To have any data deleted, email charleswolff721.2@gmail.com. It is deleted within 7 days. Data from an account whose access was removed on the platform is deleted within 30 days.

5. Changes and contact

Changes are posted on this page with a new date. Questions: charleswolff721.2@gmail.com.
